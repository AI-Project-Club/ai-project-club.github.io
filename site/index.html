Sharing, questions, support and feedback.

This is not a silent room. Expect background music, tables for collaboration, and quieter spaces for uninterrupted work.
The Townhall is the point where everyone comes back together. You can share something you learnt, ask for help, offer your perspective or advice and celebrate progress made during the session.
